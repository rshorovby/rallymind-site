How it works

Three steps, no gear and no editing

Film 10–30 seconds

One stroke, phone to the side or behind you at waist height. Your phone camera is enough — no tripod, no second person.

02

Who it's for, and who it isn't

We review video; we don't replace on-court coaching. Better to say that up front.

It fits you if

  • You play regularly and film yourself at least sometimes.
  • You want to know exactly what to fix, not just “move your feet more”.
  • You're willing to film the same stroke several times to see a trend.

It doesn't fit you if

  • You want live on-court coaching — that's a different format and we don't replace it.
  • You don't want a human coach's input — automated analysis alone is enough for you.
  • You're not comfortable filming yourself and reviewing your own technique.
Common questions

Questions

How is this different from ChatGPT or other AI analysis?
The review is made by a model that knows your context: your profile (level, experience, hand, how often you play), your earlier videos on that stroke, your current focus and recurring issues, plus a knowledge base on technique. On top of it — live feedback from a staff coach. ChatGPT has neither your history nor a coach watching the analysis.
Do I need Telegram?
For now, yes: the Telegram bot is the live channel. The iPhone app is in development — it will add push notifications when a review is ready and keep a copy of your video on the device. You can register interest from the bot: tap “Follow the iOS app”.
How long does a review take?
The AI review appears right after you upload the video. Coach feedback takes up to 24 hours, usually faster. If the coach doesn't get to it in time, the review is still delivered to you.
What should I film, and how?
10–30 seconds, one stroke, phone to the side or behind you at waist height. You don't need a whole rally: the shorter the clip, the more precise the review. The review then tells you what to film next.
How much does it cost?
The product is in open beta and reviews are free right now. When pricing arrives, the first review will stay free — you should see what the product actually gives you before paying for it.
What happens to my videos?
We don't keep videos on our server: the file is deleted after the review. In Telegram it stays in your chat with the bot — you can clear that any time.

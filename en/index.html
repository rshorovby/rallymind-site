What's inside

One stroke at a time

We take a forehand, a serve or a backhand and stay with it.

A review that fits you

A beginner and someone in their third year get different notes. We look at how long you've played, which hand you use and how often you're on court.

We remember past videos

If we've already reviewed this stroke, we check whether the last note changed anything.

A coach adds their own note

AI writes the review first. Then a coach watches the video and adds what was missing. Until they've watched, their note isn't in the review yet.

Common questions

Questions

How is this different from ChatGPT and other AI reviews?
ChatGPT hasn't seen your earlier videos and doesn't remember what you're working on. Here the review is built from your footage, and a coach watches it too.
Do I need Telegram?
For now, yes: it all happens in the bot. The iPhone app is in progress. You'll get a notification when a review is ready, and a copy of the video stays on your phone. Leave a request from the link “I want the iPhone app”.
How long does a review take?
The review arrives right after you upload. The coach's note usually comes the same day, and always within a day. If the coach doesn't get to it in time, the review still arrives.
What should I film, and how?
10–30 seconds, one stroke, phone to the side or behind you at waist height. You don't need a whole rally: a short clip is read more precisely. Then we tell you what to film next.
How much does it cost?
It's free for now. When pricing arrives, the first review stays free, so you can see whether it's worth it.
What happens to my videos?
We delete the file from our server right after the review. In Telegram it stays in the chat with the bot until you delete it.
